mpv (media player)
  Bundled as: mpv\mpv.exe (installed in the "resources\mpv" folder next to the application)
  License:    GNU General Public License, version 2 or later. The Windows build is made with
              components that may make the combined work GPL version 3.
  Website:    https://mpv.io
  Source:     https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv
  Windows build scripts and exact release: https://github.com/shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake
  (the file resources\mpv\VERSION.txt names the exact build that is included)

  mpv is a separate program. This application starts it as an external process to play video.
  You may replace the bundled copy with your own build of mpv, or choose another player
  in Settings.
